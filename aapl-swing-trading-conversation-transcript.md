# Process > Indicators — The Deep Dive

You picked up on the most important line, so let me unpack it.

## Why Indicators Don't Make Money
Every trader has access to the same RSI, the same SMAs, the same Bollinger Bands. What separates winners isn't the *signal*, it's:
1. Which signals you act on (selection)
2. How much you risk (sizing)
3. What you do when wrong (exits)
4. What you do after (review)

## The Six-Stage Swing Trading Process

**Stage 1: Universe Selection** (Weekend)
- Watchlist of 15–25 names max
- Re-scan once a week, not daily

**Stage 2: Bias Setting** (Weekly Top-Down)
- One question: "Long, short, or neutral on this stock for the next 2–8 weeks?"

**Stage 3: Setup Identification** (Daily)
- Pick three setups, stick to them:
  - Pullback to MA
  - Breakout from base
  - Failed breakdown

**Stage 4: Trigger & Entry** (4H or 1H)
- Specific event, not a feeling

**Stage 5: Position Management**
Before clicking buy, written down:
1. Entry price
2. Stop loss
3. First target
4. Second target / trail
5. Position size
6. Time stop
7. Invalidation news

**Stage 6: Post-Trade Review**
> Did I follow my plan? (Y/N — only question that matters)

## The Risk Framework

**Rule 1:** Risk 0.5–1% per trade.
**Rule 2:** Position Size = Risk ÷ Stop Distance.
**Rule 3:** Portfolio heat cap 3–4% across all open positions.

## The Brutally Honest Hierarchy

| Rank | Factor | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk management & position sizing | ~35% |
| 2 | Selectivity | ~25% |
| 3 | Patience | ~15% |
| 4 | Trade management | ~10% |
| 5 | Psychology / discipline | ~10% |
| 6 | Indicator settings | ~5% |

The thing most retail traders obsess over (#6) is the smallest factor.
